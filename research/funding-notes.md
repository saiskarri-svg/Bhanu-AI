# Funding research notes (October 2026)

Notes from the research for the "Funding for schools" box on Returns. The research tool could not open pages in full, so facts come from search results quoting each page. Spot-check the links below before relying on them.

## National

- Network limits: the research tool could not open web pages directly (the session proxy blocked page downloads). Every fact here comes from search results that quoted the named official documents. Please open each URL once before the box goes live.
- PM Surya Ghar is confirmed as households only. The residential guidelines say: 'No CFA will be provided to non-residential segments of consumers (including government segment, commercial and industrial segment etc.)'. Source: PMSG Guidelines for Residential CFA, Capex Mode, https://www.solar.delhi.gov.in/public/assets/solar/PMSG_Guidelines_for_Residential_CFA_Capex_Mode.pdf (MNRE guideline hosted by Delhi Government).
- CSR under Schedule VII is confirmed. Renewable energy is not named word for word in Schedule VII; solar fits under item (iv) environmental sustainability and item (ii) education because MCA says to read the items liberally (General Circular 14/2021, which replaced 21/2014).
- MPLADS: I could not read Annexure VIII (the works list) of the April 2023 guidelines. The 'non-conventional energy systems for community use' and school building items come from the MPLADS Pocket Book and older guidelines on mplads.gov.in. Check page 51 of the 2023 PDF. The Rs 5 crore a year figure is from general knowledge of the scheme, not from a page seen this session.
- MNRE government buildings guideline covers central government buildings, with a target date of 31 December 2025. I could not find whether that target was met, or a matching central scheme for state government schools. The pay per unit entry is a model to ask about, not a guaranteed scheme.
- 15th Finance Commission grants ended on 31 March 2026. The 16th Finance Commission grants (2026-27 to 2030-31) now apply. The untied share and conditions came from news sources, not the official report. No central advisory names solar on schools; it depends on the state's panchayat rules. The Eleventh Schedule listing education and non-conventional energy is constitutional text, cited from memory.
- DMF: whether school solar counts as 'education' (70% share) or 'energy' (30% share) is not stated; state DMF rules decide.
- Samagra Shiksha: the framework seen is marked as a draft; amounts may have changed. The 2024 letter to states mentions solar for schools without electricity.
- Left out: IREDA rooftop loans (only for developers and aggregators of 1,000 kWp or more, 2015 terms, status unknown), NABARD (nothing found for schools), Green Energy Corridor (transmission only, not for schools).
- Remember: a grid-tied system without a battery switches off during a power cut. None of these schemes changes that.

## States: Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi

- Important: this session's network blocked direct page fetches (WebFetch and curl were refused for every site, including .gov.in). All facts come from web search result summaries of the listed pages, not from pages opened in full. Please spot-check before publishing.
- Andaman and Nicobar Islands: no school or government building scheme could be confirmed. A 2022 Rajya Sabha reply lists about 4.59 MW of rooftop solar on government buildings, but no named scheme was found.
- Andhra Pradesh: the NREDCAP URL is taken from district government pages. The PM SHRI school drive is run under a central scheme by the state School Education Department.
- Arunachal Pradesh: a 2024 net metering regulation was reported, but the article used the wrong regulator name, so netMetering is left as null. Some reports place a separate 1.6 MW government building tender under PM Surya Ghar; left out.
- Assam: the 2018 AEDA 70 percent subsidy excluded government institutions, so it is not listed. The PM SHRI bid document is hosted on a news site.
- Bihar: the BREDA URL (breda.bih.nic.in) came from a third-party page and was not confirmed on an official page. The 1,890 schools figure comes from an exam-prep news summary; the Patna 1,300 buildings plan has no completion report.
- Chandigarh: CREST's own site (crestchd.org.in) appeared in results but the UT solar portal is used as the main link. The Tribune article on schools was not dated in results.
- Chhattisgarh: a CREDA media page headline mentions solar for all PM SHRI schools, but no details were found, so it is left out. The 2020 RESCO tender outcome is unknown.
- Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu: the current state nodal agency is not clear. The power company is now DNH and DD Power Distribution Corporation (Torrent Power joint venture). dded.gov.in is the UT administration site.
- Delhi: the 500 square metre rule for government buildings comes from the 2016 policy; it is not confirmed in the 2023 policy, so it is left out. The 2023 policy was notified in March 2024 and amended in 2025 and September 2026.
- All amounts, prices and capacities are rough guides.

## States: Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Ladakh

- Direct page fetches were blocked in this environment (network proxy). Every entry is based on web search extracts of the cited pages, not a full read. Re-check before publishing.
- Goa: the 50% rate and 90 kW cap for educational institutions come from press reports; the official press release lists educational institutions but the extract did not show a percentage. The 2026 budget also mentions a three-year extension, which may change the end date (stated as 31 March 2028).
- Gujarat: the 100% state-paid programme covers state government buildings. Reports do not say whether government or aided schools are included. Aided schools are likely not covered.
- Haryana: no current HAREDA scheme names schools. The 2017 school hybrid plan is old and its outcome is unknown. HAREDA also has a 50% social sector institutions scheme (hostels, orphanages, gurukuls) that does not name schools, so it was left out.
- Himachal Pradesh: a 2023 NHPC CSR project put 5 kW plants on 13 Kullu schools through HIMURJA; left out as it is CSR, not a state scheme. The 2025 RESCO tender is for 100 kW and above, so it may not suit small schools.
- Jammu and Kashmir: completion was targeted for December 2025; by May 2026 only part of a 152.75 MW RESCO tender had been awarded. Whether schools in a given district are covered is unclear.
- Jharkhand: the residential schools programme date and whether it is still running are unclear. Net metering size limits (1 kWp to 1 MWp) come from a vendor guide and were left out.
- Karnataka: the 2026 government buildings push is reported under PM Surya Ghar; it is a government buildings drive, not the household subsidy. Schools are not named explicitly.
- Kerala: net metering limits in the 2025 KSERC rules (about 3 kW, or 5 kW with battery; net billing up to 500 kW) come from draft-stage reports; the final rules reportedly eased some limits. A 3 to 10 kW school may fall under net billing. No current school-specific scheme found.
- Ladakh: LREDA's official page is on the Leh district site; there is no separate verified LREDA website. Ladakh Power Development Department (https://lpdd.ladakh.gov.in/) runs most grid-connected rooftop work. An old (about 2017) note says government offices got up to 90% subsidy and schools 50% for solar devices; it could not be verified as current, so it was left out.
- All amounts are rough guides. PM Surya Ghar household subsidy is not listed as a school scheme.

## States: Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry

- WebFetch and direct downloads were blocked in this session (DNS and proxy errors), so no page was opened in full. Every entry comes from web search results that quote the page. Treat all items as needing a quick check before publishing.
- Lakshadweep: LEDA has no website of its own that I could find; the URL given is the UT portal. The 2026 rooftop tender names the Lakshadweep Electricity Department as nodal agency, not LEDA. These systems are hybrid with batteries, which suits an island grid; JERC net metering may be limited there.
- Madhya Pradesh: a 2021 minister announcement to put solar on school roofs had no follow-up I could confirm, so it is left out. A 20% state capital subsidy for institutions was reported in 2018; I could not confirm it is still offered. A June 2025 report said all government buildings would get rooftop solar by end of 2025; not confirmed.
- Maharashtra: the Zilla Parishad school drives are Nagpur district only. No statewide school scheme found.
- Manipur: the RESCO tender and UNDP form pages have no clear dates. Manipur got its own regulator (MnERC) in July 2024; a draft 2026 rule may replace the current net metering rules.
- Meghalaya: school systems under the CM Solar Mission are off-grid with batteries, not grid-tied. Sources differ on subsidy tiers (one says 70% for households, another 90% for small systems). The SKOCH award claim was not confirmed.
- Mizoram: no official ZEDA website found, so the agency URL is empty. Unclear whether the 2017 incentive is still offered, and whether Mizoram now has its own regulator after the JERC for Manipur and Mizoram split.
- Nagaland: the year of the Riizhiikhrie school plant is not stated in the source. The 90/10 central/state funding split is from a news report about an older central programme.
- Odisha: an undated report said OREDA would light 1,670 SC/ST department schools and hostels with solar; left out as unverified.
- Puducherry: no REAP website found; URL is the government undertakings list on py.gov.in. Not confirmed that schools are in the first SECI batch.

## States: Punjab, Rajasthan, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal

- Page fetching was blocked in this environment (WebFetch and curl could not reach any site), so every source was checked through web search results and their extracts, not by opening the full page. URLs are ones that appeared in search results; none were made up.
- Tamil Nadu: TEDA was merged into Tamil Nadu Green Energy Corporation (TNGECL) in 2024. The TNGECL website was not confirmed, so the agency link points to teda.in. Green School Scheme source is a news report of an official study.
- Sikkim: SREDA website sikkimsreda.in comes from a Sikkim Power Department page. No Sikkim net metering rules for government buildings were found, so netMetering is null. The NHPC tender is a central PSU drive, status unclear.
- Rajasthan: a 2021 RERC direction to solarise about 1,500 unelectrified government schools (with CSR funds) was reported only by a coaching site, so it was left out. Net metering cap of 1 MW is from news, not the RERC order.
- Tripura: net metering cap of 10 kWp per consumer is from news of the 2025 amendment; a 10 kWp school is at the limit.
- Uttarakhand and Uttar Pradesh government building drives do not name schools; schools may need to ask through the Education Department.
- Net metering lines for Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, Uttarakhand and West Bengal rely on news or NGO summaries of regulator orders, not the gazette text.
- All amounts and sizes are rough guides from tenders and reports.

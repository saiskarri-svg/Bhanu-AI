# Website ideas by market segment (2026-10-21)

Research by the `segment-scout` agent for Sai. Ideas are numbered 41 to 58 (1 to 40 are in `.claude/ideas-log.md`).

**How this was researched:** web search only. Pages were not opened, so every fact below comes from **search result snippets** and summaries of them; check the source before using a number in a pitch. All prices, fees and market sizes are **rough guides**. Token costs are rough estimates for a first version (three screens, shared data, published to an artifact).

**Kit parts referred to below:** dark UI, charts, shared data (db collections for logs and trackers), WhatsApp alerts with limits, AI advisor (on the viewer's Claude account), PDF reports, translations (en/te/hi), sourced facts (Sources tab plus fact-checker), lessons with quizzes (Learn tab), idea and triage agents.

## Batch A

### 41. Rooftop solar installer site with home solar care
- **Who pays:** small PM Surya Ghar vendors and installers in AP and Telangana towns; users are their customers (households).
- **The job:** a branded site that gives a household a size, cost and subsidy estimate, then after installation keeps a cleaning and fault log with readings and WhatsApp reminders, so the installer can sell yearly maintenance.
- **Proof of demand:**
  - PM Surya Ghar crossed about 50 lakh rooftop installations by mid 2026, with 34,219 registered vendors (29,469 active); a Rajya Sabha answer gave a lower 41.9 lakh, so figures differ. AP had 9,12,788 applications as of 5 Aug 2026. ([Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/news-brief/pm-surya-ghar-draws-777-lakh-applications-419-lakh-rooftop-systems-installed), [Swarajya 50 lakh](https://swarajyamag.com/infrastructure/pm-surya-ghar-scheme-crosses-50-lakh-installations-as-july-delivers-record-506-lakh-additions), [KNN India](https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/sectors/pm-surya-ghar-scheme-crosses-50-lakh-rooftop-solar-installations-reaches-148-gw-capacity)) Snippets only.
  - Home solar maintenance plans already sell: about ₹1,800 a year for 4 visits on a 3 kW system (SolarSquare), ₹6,000 to ₹9,000 for 3 to 5 kW with 24 cleanings (Bridgeway), ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 per kW per year (Quickestimate). ([SolarSquare](https://www.solarsquare.in/blog/solar-panel-cleaning-pricing-in-india/), [Bridgeway](https://bridgewaypower.in/blog/solar-panel-cleaning-cost-india-amc-guide), [Quickestimate](https://quickestimate.co/blog/solar-amc-pricing-india)) Snippets only.
- **Build size:** medium (logins per home, shared data, dashboard).
- **Kit fit:** very high. Solar model and simulator, Returns tab (subsidy and payback), maintenance log, readings, risk rules and WhatsApp limits, funder PDF becomes a customer quote PDF, Telugu.
- **Risks:** subsidy amounts change (label as rough guide, link to the national portal); no-battery systems switch off in power cuts (keep the house rule); installers may want their own branding; customer phone numbers are personal data.
- **First version:** (1) Quote: roof size and bill to system size, cost, subsidy, payback; (2) My system: readings, cleaning log, faults with risk level; (3) Installer view: all homes, due cleanings, high risk list with WhatsApp.

### 52. NGO and CSR impact site
- **Who pays:** NGOs that take CSR money; sometimes the CSR team of the company. Users are NGO staff and funders.
- **The job:** a public impact page plus a private tracker of activities, beneficiaries and spending, with a one-click funder report PDF.
- **Proof of demand:**
  - India CSR spending about ₹40,794 crore in FY2024-25 from 29,546 companies (Fulcrum report); other estimates are lower, about ₹25,000 to ₹28,000 crore. Weak outcome tracking is a known problem. ([UNI India](https://www.uniindia.com/india-inc-s-spending-on-csr-hits-rs-40-794-cr-in-fy2024-25-report/business-economy/news/3913828.html), [Smile Foundation](https://www.smilefoundationindia.org/blog/csr-spending-india-2026/)) Snippets only.
  - NGOs need CSR-1 registration plus 80G and 12A, and funders ask for output reports (for example Dentsu reporting 72,000 students across 27 NGO partners). ([Vayam](https://vayam.org.in/csr-funds-india-how-ngos-utilise-donations/), [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/report/dentsu-98-million-csr-spend-10607146)) Snippets only.
- **Build size:** medium.
- **Kit fit:** high. Impact tab, funder PDF and pitch-pack skill, charts, sourced facts and fact-checker, translations, shared logs.
- **Risks:** beneficiary data privacy (children, health); claims must be true (fact-checker helps); NGOs have small budgets, so price per project or bill the CSR grant.
- **First version:** (1) Public impact page with live numbers; (2) Activity and spending log; (3) Funder report PDF builder.

### 42. Solar for schools abroad (Bhanu AI for NGOs in Africa and elsewhere)
- **Who pays:** NGOs, solar donors and installers lighting up schools in Africa; also school energy programmes in the UK.
- **The job:** Bhanu AI made general: one page per school with output, savings, maintenance log, lessons for pupils, and donor reports.
- **Proof of demand:**
  - Estimates say 65% to 80% of schools in sub-Saharan Africa lack basic electricity; Rwanda alone listed 551 schools without power; charities such as SolarAid and Energy 4 Impact run school solar programmes. ([Energy 4 Impact](https://energy4impact.org/impact/energy-4-education), [Waltham Forest Echo](https://walthamforestecho.co.uk/2022/05/16/walthamstow-charity-pledges-light-for-every-sub-saharan-school-by-2030/), [New Times Rwanda](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/25275/Opinions/building-classrooms-making-quality-education-a-reality/amp), [Gambiana](https://gambiana.com/british-engineering-students-installed-solar-power-at-a-school-in-kembujeh-village/)) Snippets only; several sources are 2012 to 2025.
  - UK schools pay for energy dashboards: Energy Sparks about £545 + VAT a year, Leicester's package £795 a year; the DfE plans school energy dashboards. ([Sheffield Hallam](https://research.shu.ac.uk/sustainability/2024/05/01/free-access-to-energy-sparks-for-selected-sheffield-schools-helping-your-school-save-energy/), [Leicester](https://schools.leicester.gov.uk/services/environment-health-and-well-being/sustainable-schools/current-projects/bess-energy/), [UKAuthority](https://ukauthority.com/articles/dfe-plans-to-give-schools-energy-dashboards)) Snippets only.
- **Build size:** medium (mostly re-skinning Bhanu AI, new country data).
- **Kit fit:** very high; whole site carries over. Off-grid schools in Africa usually have batteries, so the power cut wording changes.
- **Risks:** different currencies, sun data and languages (French, Swahili); finding the first NGO partner; payment from abroad.
- **First version:** (1) School overview with output and savings in local currency; (2) Maintenance and readings; (3) Donor report PDF.

## Batch B

### 47. Coaching centre and tuition manager
- **Who pays:** tuition and coaching centre owners in small towns; parents and students use it.
- **The job:** batches, attendance, fees due, test marks with charts, and WhatsApp notes to parents within limits; practice quizzes.
- **Proof of demand:** vendors charge ₹5 to ₹12 per student a month (Vidhya Aahvan) or from ₹999 (Coachplus); many charge per centre on request. ([GoodFirms](https://www.goodfirms.co/software/vidhya-aahvan), [Capterra India](https://www.capterra.in/software/1107960/Coachplus), [Agamedu](https://agamedu.in/)) Snippets only.
- **Build size:** medium.
- **Kit fit:** high. Learn quizzes and certificates, savings tracker becomes fee tracker, WhatsApp limits, charts, Telugu.
- **Risks:** crowded market, so win on Telugu, simplicity and price; children's data; fee collection needs a payment link (UPI) later.
- **First version:** (1) Today: batches, absentees, fees due; (2) Student card: attendance, marks chart, fees; (3) Parent message with limits.

### 48. Budget private school website and notice board
- **Who pays:** owners of low-fee private schools; parents read it.
- **The job:** a fast, bilingual school site with admissions enquiry, notices, events, results and a fee reminder list.
- **Proof of demand:** 3,39,583 private unaided schools in India in 2024-25 (3,41,689 in 2025-26), with 8.57 crore pupils; no AP split found. ([Education for All in India](https://educationforallinindia.com/from-classrooms-to-policy-analysing-udise-2024-25-data/), [UDISE+ booklet](https://dashboard.udiseplus.gov.in/report-new-v.6-demo2025/static/media/UDISE%2B2024_25_Booklet_existing.118ba29d4773e6372f72.pdf)) Snippets only. No source on what schools pay for websites.
- **Build size:** small to medium.
- **Kit fit:** high. Tabbed shell, Mission and Impact tabs, translations, shared notices, WhatsApp.
- **Risks:** price-sensitive buyers; photos of children need consent.
- **First version:** (1) Home and admissions enquiry; (2) Notices and events; (3) Office view: enquiries and fee reminders.

### 46. Small clinic site with appointments and reminders
- **Who pays:** single-doctor clinics, dentists, physiotherapists in towns.
- **The job:** clinic page, appointment requests, a day list, and WhatsApp reminders (within limits) for follow-ups and vaccines.
- **Proof of demand:** Indian clinic software with WhatsApp reminders costs about ₹799 to ₹1,999 a month (Careflow AI, Qare, Clinizy Care). ([Techjockey Careflow](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/careflow-ai), [SaaSworthy Clinizy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/clinizy-care/pricing), [GetApp Qare](https://www.getapp.ie/software/2083678/qare-1)) Snippets only; vendor prices.
- **Build size:** medium.
- **Kit fit:** medium. Shared data, WhatsApp limits, Telugu, charts for visits. The AI advisor must not give medical advice.
- **Risks:** health data is sensitive (DPDP Act); keep only names, times and phone numbers, no diagnoses, in a first version; doctors' trust.
- **First version:** (1) Clinic page and booking request; (2) Today's list; (3) Reminder list with WhatsApp.

## Batch C

### 43. Shrimp and fish pond log (AP aquaculture)
- **Who pays:** shrimp farmers in West and East Godavari, Krishna, Nellore; or feed and input dealers who give it to farmers.
- **The job:** daily pond log (water readings, feed, deaths), charts by day of crop, and high risk alerts when readings go out of range.
- **Proof of demand:** AP is India's top shrimp producer; FarmMOJO has about 6,000 farmers across AP, Gujarat and Tamil Nadu and charges about ₹2,000 per pond per crop; ICAR-CIBA runs a Bhimavaram lab and the Vanami Shrimpapp. ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/enterprise/big-idea/shrimp-farmer-finds-mojo-5654), [ICAR](https://icar.org.in/node/11094)) Snippets only. No current AP farmer count found.
- **Build size:** medium.
- **Kit fit:** high. Readings collection, risk rules (High, Medium, Low), WhatsApp limits, charts, Telugu, triage agent.
- **Risks:** safe ranges must be sourced from ICAR/CIBA (fact-checker); disease advice is risky, so point to labs; seasonal; competition from FarmMOJO.
- **First version:** (1) Ponds overview with risk dots; (2) Daily entry form; (3) Pond chart and alerts.

### 44. FPO (farmer producer organisation) dashboard
- **Who pays:** FPOs and the agencies that support them (CBBOs, NGOs).
- **The job:** members, shares, input sales, produce collected, buyer prices and a board report PDF.
- **Proof of demand:** all 10,000 FPOs under the central scheme are registered (target met by Dec 2025); many struggle with viability and records; FPOs are being linked to e-NAM, ONDC and GeM. No FPO software prices found. ([AgroSpectrum](https://agrospectrumindia.com/news/24/33060/indias-fpo-push-gains-momentum-as-10000-collectives-go-national-with-credit-digital-and-market-linkages.html), [Vision IAS](https://visionias.in/current-affairs/upsc-daily-news-summary/article/2025-03-17/business-standard/schemes-in-news/10000-fpos-is-not-too-many-but-their-effectiveness-remains-in-question), [data.gov.in](https://www.data.gov.in/resource/stateut-wise-number-registered-farmer-producer-organizations-fpos-under-central-sector)) Snippets only.
- **Build size:** medium.
- **Kit fit:** medium to high. Shared logs, charts, funder PDF becomes board and bank report, Telugu, AI advisor for crop and market questions.
- **Risks:** money records need care (not accounting software); buyer is often a grant; data entry habits.
- **First version:** (1) Members and shares; (2) Inputs and produce log; (3) Monthly report PDF.

### 45. Village dairy collection centre
- **Who pays:** milk collection centre operators and small dairy societies.
- **The job:** morning and evening milk entries with fat and SNF, a rate chart, each farmer's 10-day payout, and a WhatsApp slip.
- **Proof of demand:** Hamari Dairy from ₹139 a month, DairyPro ₹2,499 a month per centre with WhatsApp briefs and offline use, MilKing app with fat and SNF rate charts. ([GetApp Hamari Dairy](https://www.getapp.ca/software/2142484/hamari-dairy), [DairyPro](https://dairyproerp.lovable.app/), [App Store MilKing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6747752855)) Snippets only.
- **Build size:** medium to large (works offline, many entries a day).
- **Kit fit:** medium. Shared data, charts, WhatsApp, Telugu; offline use is new work.
- **Risks:** payouts must be exact; poor network in villages; existing cheap apps.
- **First version:** (1) Today's entries; (2) Farmer payout slip; (3) Rate chart settings.

## Batch D

### 49. Gram panchayat works and funds board
- **Who pays:** panchayats (untied grants), MLAs, district officials or CSR; citizens use it.
- **The job:** public board of works (photo, cost, status), funds received, and a grievance list sorted by risk, in Telugu.
- **Proof of demand:** 12,853 eligible AP gram panchayats in one 15th Finance Commission release, with about ₹395 crore untied and ₹593 crore tied grants; eGramSwaraj covers planning and accounts but is not a simple public board. ([The News Minute](https://thenewsminute.com/andhra-pradesh/andhra-pradesh-receives-union-grants-of-rs-98873-cr), [News on AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/centre-releases-grants-worth-%e2%82%b92255-crore-for-rural-development-in-andhra-pradesh), [OpenGov Asia](https://opengovasia.com/india-reviews-rural-local-body-grants-under-fifteenth-finance-commission/)) Snippets only.
- **Build size:** medium.
- **Kit fit:** high. Repair log becomes works log, maintenance triage becomes grievance triage, WhatsApp limits, translations, charts, sourced facts.
- **Risks:** politics; slow government buying; must not look like an official government site (no state emblem or branding).
- **First version:** (1) Works board; (2) Funds received and spent chart; (3) Raise a grievance and the triage list.

### 50. SHG books and scam safety
- **Who pays:** NGOs, banks (financial literacy budgets), CSR; users are self help group leaders and members.
- **The job:** simple group books (savings, loans, repayments) with a monthly sheet, plus short Telugu lessons and quizzes on UPI scams and loans.
- **Proof of demand:** AP leads SHG bank linkage with ₹15,229 crore credit (Sept 2026) and plans digital reforms to stop fake memberships; about 8.5 lakh groups with about 1 crore women (older World Bank figure). 20% of UPI-using families hit by fraud since 2022 (LocalCircles); rural cyber fraud rising. ([Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/andhra-pradesh/ap-tops-india-for-linking-shg-bank-linkage-with-15229-crore-credit-disbursement-1992598), [World Bank blog](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/endpovertyinsouthasia/accountability-alchemy), [The Logical Indian](https://thelogicalindian.com/1-in-5-indian-upi-users-hit-by-fraud-since-2022-survey-rbi-npci-launch-new-safeguards-from-june-30/), [ORF](https://www.orfonline.org/expert-speak/digital-deception-in-rural-india-and-the-ai-counter-offensive)) Snippets only; figures differ by year.
- **Build size:** medium.
- **Kit fit:** high. Learn lessons and quizzes, certificate, savings tracker, translations, fact-checker.
- **Risks:** SERP already runs official bookkeeping, so position as training and a helper, not a replacement; money data; must not give investment advice.
- **First version:** (1) Group books; (2) Scam safety lessons and quiz; (3) Certificate and group report.

### 58. Heat-safe schools
- **Who pays:** district education offices, CSR, NGOs; schools and panchayats use it.
- **The job:** daily heat level for the school, a heat action checklist (water, shade, timings), incident log, and WhatsApp alerts on red days within limits.
- **Proof of demand:** UP told schools in 2026 to keep heat action plans, watch IMD alerts and log heat incidents; Telangana's 2026 heatwave plan says 1.6 crore people live in critical zones. No AP school plan found. ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/uttar-pradesh/up-launches-school-heat-action-plan-to-shield-students-from-extreme-heat), [The South First](https://thesouthfirst.com/health/amid-orange-alerts-in-18-districts-telangana-action-plan-warns-1-6-crore-people-in-critical-heatwave-zones)) Snippets only.
- **Build size:** small to medium.
- **Kit fit:** high. Dust outlook becomes heat outlook, checklist and Today strip, risk rules, WhatsApp limits, Learn safety quiz. Pairs well with Bhanu AI schools.
- **Risks:** weather data source and its terms; must not replace official IMD warnings; seasonal (March to June).
- **First version:** (1) Today's heat level and actions; (2) Checklist and incident log; (3) Alert settings.

## Batch E

### 53. Village homestay and tribal tourism site
- **Who pays:** homestay owners (Araku, Lambasingi), tribal cooperatives, tour guides.
- **The job:** homestay pages with photos, availability calendar, enquiry by WhatsApp, local guides and trails, in English, Telugu and Hindi.
- **Proof of demand:** AP has identified 500+ homestays; ASR district plans 40,000 homestays; the state runs a homestay registration portal (Silver or Gold class, 2-year certificate). ([Outlook Traveller](https://www.outlooktraveller.com/News/500-homestays-to-push-sustainable-tourism-initiatives-in-andhra-pradesh), [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/amp/southern-states/andhra-pradesh/asr-district-plans-40000-home-stays-to-boost-tribal-tourism-1915165), [AP homestays portal](https://homestays.ap.gov.in/)) Snippets only.
- **Build size:** medium.
- **Kit fit:** medium. Dark UI suits photos, translations, shared calendar, WhatsApp. Also sells to foreign visitors.
- **Risks:** online payments and cancellations; listing sites (Airbnb, MakeMyTrip) compete; owners need registration.
- **First version:** (1) Stay page with photos; (2) Availability and enquiry; (3) Owner calendar.

### 54. Function hall and event booking
- **Who pays:** function hall and kalyana mandapam owners, caterers, decorators.
- **The job:** availability calendar with muhurtham dates, enquiries, advance and balance tracker, vendor list.
- **Proof of demand:** Indian banquet software lists at ₹15,000 one-time (Meshink) to about ₹7,100 a month (Blynk); NextEvent says 135+ venues track muhurtham dates. ([Techjockey banquet](https://techjockey.com/category/banquet-management), [Product Hunt NextEvent](https://www.producthunt.com/products/nexteventapp-com)) Snippets only; prices may be dated.
- **Build size:** small to medium.
- **Kit fit:** medium. Shared data, savings tracker as payments tracker, Telugu, PDF receipts.
- **Risks:** money disputes, so receipts must be clear; seasonal.
- **First version:** (1) Calendar; (2) Enquiry and booking card; (3) Payments due.

### 55. Plot layout sales site
- **Who pays:** small developers selling DTCP or RERA approved plots.
- **The job:** an interactive layout map with plot status (open, booked, sold), approval numbers shown, site visit booking.
- **Proof of demand:** buyers are told to check the layout number on the DTCP portal and treat missing layouts as unapproved; APCRDA's Amaravati layout shows DTCP and RERA numbers; no live plot maps found from the state. ([Assetly](https://assetlyhq.com/blog/how-to-check-layout-approval-valid-india/), [APCRDA FAQ](https://crda.ap.gov.in/crda_norifications/NOT01098212/01~FAQ%20on%20Amaravati%20MIG%20Layout.pdf), [Homes247](https://www.homes247.in/property/tirupati/yerpedu/om-sai-balasa-chakrapani-nagar-14418)) Snippets only. No price data found.
- **Build size:** medium.
- **Kit fit:** medium. Dark UI, interactive diagram (like the illustrated school), shared status, sourced approvals.
- **Risks:** trust and fraud: show only approvals that can be checked; RERA advertising rules; do not promise returns.
- **First version:** (1) Layout map; (2) Plot card with approvals; (3) Site visit request and sales view.

## Batch F

### 56. NRI property care
- **Who pays:** Telugu NRIs in the US, UK, Gulf and Australia with homes or plots in AP and Telangana.
- **The job:** monthly inspection log with photos, rent and bills, repairs with costs, high risk alerts and a yearly PDF.
- **Proof of demand:** property management fees in India are about 5% to 15% of rent or ₹1,500 to ₹5,000 a month (Bajaj Finserv); an NRI plan lists ₹4,499 a month (Chennai); Hyderabad firms offer NRI services without public prices. ([Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/what-is-property-management-and-its-work), [Address Advisors](https://addressadvisors.com/services/property-management-services-in-hyderabad), [Trustpilot Probity PM](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/probitypm.in)) Snippets only.
- **Build size:** medium (photo uploads).
- **Kit fit:** high. Repair log, maintenance triage, WhatsApp, PDF, readings-style monthly log.
- **Risks:** the website is a tool for a service someone must run on the ground; trust; tenant data.
- **First version:** (1) Property overview; (2) Inspection and repair log; (3) Owner report PDF.

### 57. Telugu for diaspora kids
- **Who pays:** Telugu parents abroad (US, UK, Australia, Gulf).
- **The job:** short Telugu lessons with letters, audio, quizzes, progress and certificates, plus live class sign-up.
- **Proof of demand:** SiliconAndhra ManaBadi reports about 10,500 students in 35 US states and 12 countries (self-reported); Preply lists 53 Telugu tutors at about $3 to $40 a lesson. ([Idealist SiliconAndhra](https://www.idealist.org/pt/ong/643788e6bd8140e1b5f0ff31c897b7d0-silicon-andhra-milpitas), [Preply](https://preply.com/en/classes/telugu?page=3)) Snippets only.
- **Build size:** medium.
- **Kit fit:** medium to high. Learn lessons, quizzes, certificates, Telugu text already in the kit.
- **Risks:** needs a good teacher and audio; payments from abroad; competes with free community schools.
- **First version:** (1) Letter lessons with quizzes; (2) Progress and certificate; (3) Class sign-up.

### 51. Local trades booking (electricians, plumbers, AC and solar technicians)
- **Who pays:** a single tradesperson or small team in a town.
- **The job:** a one-page site with services and rough prices, booking request, job log with photos and a WhatsApp job slip.
- **Proof of demand:** weak direct evidence found in this search; the 34,219 PM Surya Ghar vendors (idea 41) show a large pool of solar technicians needing service tools. Not separately sourced.
- **Build size:** small to medium.
- **Kit fit:** medium. Repair log, risk rules, WhatsApp, Telugu.
- **Risks:** low willingness to pay; Urban Company and Justdial compete; needs proof of demand before building.
- **First version:** (1) Services page; (2) Booking request; (3) Job log.

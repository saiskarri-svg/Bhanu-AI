# GridSight change log

Newest first. One line per change. Artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/PZNH8ccqmgMXbZZmybJqXS

## Site at a glance

- **App:** `app/` (React, Vite, Tailwind; copy of the Site Studio starter). Tabs: Map (landing), Site, Demand, Money (Size and tariff, Funding, Compare villages), Sun and weather, Trust (Checks, Data sharing, Method and sources); old #growth, #funding, #validation, #data-sharing, #method links still open the right section. Shared site settings bar (village, year, size, households, tariff) with the answer in five numbers on every tab, saved in the browser (`components/settings.tsx`), and jump links to each box; headline and 5-step strip on every tab; compact village bar after Site. Light/Dark toggle (follows system, remembered).
- **Data:** `app/data/*.json`, one per sample village (Gbamu Gbamu NG, Kychom SL, Benichab MR, Talek KE, Kitobo UG, Bulongwa TZ), 1 km radius, made by `pipeline/gridsight_fetch.py` (`pipeline/fetch_samples.sh` rebuilds all).
- **Demand engine (main model):** Fereshteh's `forecast/demand_forecast.py`, run on every village by `pipeline/run_engine.py` into `app/data/engine/*.json`; read by `app/components/engine.ts`.
- **Building-level model (second opinion):** `app/components/model.ts` (classification, parameters, Monte Carlo range, drivers, growth, sizing). Sources in `app/components/sources.ts`.
- **Held-out test:** `pipeline/holdout_tags.py` writes `app/data/holdout/tags.json` (OSM-tagged buildings from other villages) for the Validation tab.
- **Pitch kit:** `PITCH.md` (3-minute script), `IMPACT.md` (one-page impact estimate).
- **Fereshteh's toolkit:** `forecast/` (village_landuse.py maps a village, demand_forecast.py gives a 5-year forecast with ranges, HOMER 8760 files, sizing and tariff risk, validate command; ranges in assumptions.yaml). Benichab, Mauritania example in `forecast/examples/`.
- **Settlements nearby:** `pipeline/cluster.py` writes `app/data/cluster/*.json` (shared-plant check).
- **Measured sites:** `pipeline/measured_sites.csv` (nearby calibration within 1,000 miles; add partner or Zenodo rows here).
- **Publish:** `./publish.sh`, then publish `app/dist/page.html` with the files in `app/dist/assets/`.

## 2026-11-01

- **Goal on the Map view:** one-line goal at the top of the left panel on desktop; on phones a Goal card under the map with Site settings directly beneath it.
- **Names and highlight:** individual names removed from the site (footer now "Built by the GridSight team"; engine and clear-sky model credited to GridSight in the app, data files and pipeline). Make it pencil out: no outline box; grants at or under $433 a connection in green, above in amber.
- **Shared plant and existing plant:** `pipeline/cluster.py` finds settlements of 50+ roofs within 10 km of each village (Google Open Buildings, 100 m grid) into `app/data/cluster/`; `components/Shared.tsx` (Money > Size and tariff) compares separate mini-grids with one shared plant plus medium-voltage line along the shortest route, using ESMAP / World Bank scale effects ($68 less per customer per 100 more customers; $100/kW portfolio saving for 5+ grids; floor at half the village's cost, assumption). New setting for villages with a mapped solar plant (Gbamu Gbamu, 85 kWp): "Build on it" counts its panels toward each stage (saves new panels at the chosen price basis).
- **Size note:** "What P25, P50, P90 mean" fold-out under the System size buttons (settings bar and Map panel), `SizeNote` in `components/settings.tsx`.
- **Right size** (`components/rightsize.ts`, `RightSize.tsx`, top of Money > Size and tariff): each year's demand as a lognormal fitted to the engine's P10/P50/P90; every stage (2, 3 or yearly) gets the smallest size that keeps brownout odds at 15% or less in each year it serves with the least idle kit. "Stages" now uses this plan (multiple expansions, wires for homes connected by each stage, results-based grant paid per connection as homes connect). Year-by-year odds against one P50 build, an all-villages table, service-against-money comparison, and a meter-based expansion trigger (add the next stage when sales stay above 80% of installed for two months). Cash flow and payback now use expected sales across the demand range (shortfalls lost, spare capacity unpaid) instead of the median only.
- **Payback period:** `components/lifetime.ts` runs 20 years (demand to year 5, then 2% a year (assumption); year-3 expansion on the same loan terms; battery and inverter renewal at 0.9 of price after 10 years; loan repayment) and gives the owner's payback (point after which the running total stays positive) and the whole-project payback. `Payback.tsx` chart on Funding; payback in the answer strip, Compare villages and the concept note; Make it pencil out now also needs the owner's money back within 5, 7, 10 or 15 years and shows the payback for each option.
- **Make it pencil out:** new Build cost option "2024 kit" (IRENA 2024 panel and battery prices x 1.5 remote-site premium, an assumption, plus 40% for wires and soft costs); "Stages" system size (first stage for year-2 demand, wires for the homes connected by year 2, the rest added in year 3); loan terms (commercial 12%/7 yrs, concessional 6%/12 yrs with 12-month grace; rough guides) and the grant are now shared settings; `components/Pencil.tsx` shows, lever by lever and at three tariffs, the smallest grant per connection that makes year-2 bills cover running costs and 1.2x the loan payment, and applies a choice with one tap. Table row lines fixed (were too bright: `border-line/60` does nothing with var colours).
- **Wires and the grid** (`components/wires.tsx`, Money > Size and tariff): village low-voltage network length from the roofs (shortest tree joining them, spans over 500 m left off) priced at $4,250 to $15,475 per km (central $14,980, ESMAP); line to the nearest mapped grid at $7,000 to $50,000 per km (central $25,000) against the solar and battery cost, with a break-even distance. Also in Compare villages (two new columns) and the concept note. Sources: ESMAP tables, OnSSET West Africa 2026, World Bank 2019 via Power Africa, RMI 2018.
- **Step strip removed** (the top tabs do the same job). **Build cost switch** in the settings bar and Map panel: 2021 engine prices or AMDA Benchmarking Africa's Minigrids 2024 (2022 to 2024 data, published 2025): about $1,046 a connection in West and Central Africa, $1,948 in East and Southern Africa, scaled by system size; feeds the answer strip, Funding, cash flow, Compare and the concept note. IRENA Renewable Power Generation Costs in 2024 added as the equipment-price reference. Cash-flow wording now matches the numbers (some sites cover their loan at P25).
- **Less scrolling, more use (batches A to F):** long explanations fold behind small toggles (`More` in `ui.tsx`); "Source:" lines are now compact source chips (`Cite`); six tabs instead of nine with sub-tabs; one settings bar for every tab plus the Map panel, with the answer in five numbers; jump links; Weather shows 4 typical-day months (worst for the battery plus seasons) with "Show all 12"; two-counts and mapped-type tables fold. New: 24-month cash flow on Funding (`CashFlow.tsx`: bills against running costs and a 70% loan after the grant; rough-guide terms), 2-page concept note PDF (`conceptNote.ts`, jspdf, uses the page's downloads capability), Compare villages (`Compare.tsx`, sortable portfolio table). Phones: settings fold behind one line and a bottom bar keeps demand, system and cost cover in view. Bhanu AI follow-ups saved in `.claude/ideas-log.md`.
- **Text alignment:** `StatusDot` has a `hang` option: when a coloured dot starts a line it sits in the margin (beside the first line) so the text lines up with the text above. Used for line-start dots on the Site, Validation and Funding tabs and in the Site Studio starter.
- **Funding tab:** new `app/components/FundingPage.tsx` (tab 8, step 7 "Find funding"). Lists funding routes for the chosen village's country (Nigeria from Sai's partner shortlist: REA DARES, Africa Minigrids Program, All On, AECF, GFIF; plus Sierra Leone, Mauritania, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania programmes) and Africa-wide ones (Mission 300, Universal Energy Facility, AMP, SEFA, GET.invest, AECF). Shows a rough grant-per-connection check against the P50 build cost and a list of what funders ask for against what GridSight already gives.

## 2026-10-31

- **Site settings panel:** the Map view's right panel is now "Site settings": village, year after connection, system size, households from roofs or Meta's map, tariff, and map style (Flat, 3D, Satellite). The left panel shows the results for those choices; the floating map toggle is gone.
- **WorldCover, sizing basis, pitch refresh:** `pipeline/landcover.py` (ESA WorldCover 2021, 10 m) adds land cover within 1 and 5 km (Site tab, context only; Meta Relative Wealth Index is not reachable here). `run_engine.py` sizes the P50 system both on the dull-season month (used) and on the yearly average, each run through 10 weather years (Growth and money: "Size for the dull season or the yearly average?"). PITCH.md and IMPACT.md rewritten with current numbers (weather, nearby meters, two household counts, staging at Gbamu Gbamu matching the built 85 kWp at year 2).

- **Two household counts side by side:** `pipeline/meta_population.py` reads Meta's HRSL population (30 m, CC BY 4.0, AWS) within each village radius; `run_engine.py` runs the engine a second time from that population (`by_population`). Demand tab: "Two ways to count households" (households, customers, energy, peak and P50 system from roofs and from Meta); Map panel line with both counts. Roofs stay the main count.

- **Fereshteh's clear-sky PV model:** `forecast/clearsky_pv.py` and `test_clearsky.py` added (her toolkit update; calibration hook kept in demand_forecast.py). New `pipeline/pv_hourly.py` runs it for every village with altitude from Copernicus 30 m elevation and NASA POWER temperatures, and scales each month by measured sunlight against clear sky and by dust on panels (agrees with weather.py within 2 to 10%). `run_engine.py` lays the year-5 hourly demand over the P50 system's typical day per month (battery need against usable size). Sun and weather tab: "A typical day in each month", 12 small charts.

- **Sharper satellite pictures:** built from Sentinel-2 bands 4, 3, 2 (not the preview TCI), median of the 2 or 3 clearest recent pictures, one haze floor for all colours, natural saturation, 960 px; the picture fades as you zoom past its 10 m detail (Map flat and 3D, Site map) so the footprints take over. Checked Maxar Open Data (sub-metre, CC BY-NC, disaster events only): no event covers the six villages.

- **Sun and weather:** `pipeline/weather.py` pulls 10 years of daily NASA POWER data per village (sunlight, clear-sky sunlight, cloud, dust and haze AOD, rain, temperature; AWS open data) and five CMIP6 models (SSP2-4.5, 2027 to 2036 against 2015 to 2024; Google Cloud). Solar output per kWp includes system, heat and dust-on-panel losses (washed by rain or monthly cleaning). Fed into the engine: monthly temperature, design sun hours (third-worst month), performance ratio, day-to-day swing, battery days. `run_engine.py` runs each P25 to P90 system day by day through the 10 weather years (and the outlook) for unserved demand and short days. New tab Sun and weather; weather line on the Map panel; NASA POWER and CMIP6 in Sources.

- **Satellite pictures:** `pipeline/satellite.py` picks the most recent cloud-free Copernicus Sentinel-2 picture (10 m, AWS open data, checked cloud-free over the village itself) and cuts it to each village (`app/data/imagery/`, Feb to Oct 2026). Shown under the Map view (Flat and 3D, Satellite switch) and the Site map, with date and Copernicus credit; inlined into the build. Google satellite imagery is not open, so not used.

- **Map view (UI batches 2 and 3):** 3D buildings (three.js, footprints extruded to typical heights per class, orbit and tilt, callouts follow the camera; Flat/3D switch, 3D by default on wide screens); building outlines now stored in site files (`outlines`, decimetres). Left panel: year picker 1 to 5, donut of who uses the power, gauge of demand against what the chosen system makes (P10 to P90 band), size picker P25 to P90 with cost, brownout, idle and cost per kWh.

- **Map view (UI batch 1):** new first tab, full screen map of the village (roads, power lines, buildings by class, mapped plant), left summary panel (year 1 and 5 demand, who uses the power, peak, customers, P50 system, cost, brownout and cost-recovery odds, grid and nearby meters), village rail on the right with year-5 demand, callouts on homes, shops, the likeliest clinic or school and the solar plant (`components/MapView.tsx`, `--map-*` tokens). Phone: map on top, compact callouts, rail and panel below.

# Kesho AI: pitch video script (about 4 minutes)

Record with a phone or screen recorder. Show the deck (Kesho_AI_Pitch_Deck.pdf) and switch to the live site for the demo part. Speak slowly; about 130 words a minute.

**0:00 to 0:30 · The problem, and one fact** (deck slide 1, then 2)
"We're Kesho AI, on Brief 03: predict mini-grid demand before building. 490 million people need mini-grids by 2030, but at today's pace only about 44,800 of the 217,000 needed get built, and each one is sized from guesses. Measured use runs 5 to 35 kWh per customer a month. Size it too big and the tariff prices people out; too small and it browns out every evening."

**0:30 to 1:10 · The solution and how it works** (slide 3)
"Kesho means tomorrow in Swahili. Kesho AI shows a village's power needs before anyone builds. It counts every roof from satellite data, forecasts five years of demand as a range across 4,000 futures, checks it against metered mini-grids within 1,000 miles and ten years of real weather, then sizes the system in stages and works out who pays."

**1:10 to 2:20 · Demo of what we built this weekend** (live site)
- Map view, Gbamu Gbamu, Nigeria: "1,346 roofs, each classed by likely use. Settings on the right change every number."
- Demand tab: "124 kWh a day in year 1, 595 by year 5, with the range and what drives it."
- Money, Right size: "One P50 build has a 37% chance of brownouts by year 5 and sits mostly idle early. Our staged plan keeps it at 15% or less."
- Money, Funding, Make it pencil out: "The smallest grant per connection that makes it pay back."
- Sun and weather, battery box: "What the battery has to carry, hour by hour."

**2:20 to 2:55 · Feasibility and cost, 6 to 18 months, who pays** (slide 4)
"Build cost is about $1,000 to $2,000 a connection. Homes pay about $5 to $8 a month, inside the $5 to $20 they spend today on kerosene and batteries. Results-based grants and concessional loans cover the build; with staging and today's equipment prices the owner is paid back in four to six years without ongoing grants. In 6 months we pilot at Gbamu Gbamu, by 12 months we calibrate on 50 to 200 metered sites, by 18 months we plug into REA and SEforALL pipelines."

**2:55 to 3:20 · Scale** (slide 5)
"Every dataset we use covers all of Africa, so any village runs in minutes on a laptop. Bundling nearby villages cuts cost per customer: at Gbamu Gbamu, sharing one plant saves about 1.1 million dollars."

**3:20 to 3:45 · Community fit and impact**
"Across six villages in six countries, 2,888 households connect by year 5. We know the forecast is grounded: the 85 kWp plant actually built at Gbamu Gbamu matches our year-2 demand, and we tested building classes on 2,225 tagged buildings."

**3:45 to 4:00 · Team and ask**
"We're the Kesho AI team [add names and roles]. We need a pilot developer, meter data from 50 to 200 sites, and an introduction to REA Nigeria. Thank you."

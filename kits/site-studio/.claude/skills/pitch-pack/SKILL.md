# Pitch pack

## Inputs (ask for any that are missing)

- **Who it is for:** the company, funder or customer, and what they care about.
- **The ask:** money, a contract, a pilot, an introduction.
- **The numbers** for this client, worked out with the site's own formulas so they match what the reader will see.

## Output

1. **One-page summary:** the problem, what the site or service does, three numbers, the cost and the ask. If the site has a PDF export, point to it.
2. **Email under 150 words:** a subject that names the client and the ask, what the money or contract buys, three numbers, one line of proof, a clear ask, and the link if the owner wants it shared (remind them a private artifact must be shared first).
3. **Five talking points,** one sentence each.

## Rules

Plain words, no em dashes. Prices and estimates are rough guides. Keep every number's source. Never make a claim the house rules in `CLAUDE.md` forbid.
